## 2. In-Depth Audit of the Milestone 7 Synthetic Claims

### 2.1. Why the M7 Results Were Not Genuine Historical Alpha
Milestone 7 documented:
- 102 OOS Trades
- 79.41% Win Rate
- 8.35 Profit Factor
- +0.9626 R Net Expectancy

Our Stage 2 source-code audit proved that these numbers were generated by running `app/research/walk_forward.py::run_100_trade_out_of_sample_study()`. This function executes against `generate_multi_day_dataset()`, which sets:
```python
is_breakout = rnd.random() < 0.78  # Artificial 78% win rate injected directly into candle geometry
```
When a simulated price series is hardcoded to rally +3.6% on 78% of simulated trading days, an ORB strategy that buys the breakout and trails with a +2.5% activation will inevitably exhibit a ~79% win rate.

### 2.2. Legitimate Value of the Synthetic Engine
While synthetic data cannot be cited as proof of commercial profitability, the synthetic study served an essential software engineering purpose:
1. **Stress-Testing Engine Invariants:** It proved that the `EventDrivenBacktester`, `PaperBroker`, `NSECostEngine`, and `PortfolioLedger` can process hundreds of trades sequentially across multiple simulated months without memory leaks, state corruption, or negative cash balance violations.
2. **Slippage Sensitivity Monotonicity:** It verified that trading performance degrades monotonically as slippage friction triples from 0.025% to 0.075%, proving that the backtester correctly applies adverse fill pricing.
3. **Statutory Cost Drag Validation:** It confirmed that statutory NSE costs (STT, stamp duty, turnover, SEBI, GST) correctly deduct ~8.1% to 8.5% of total risk budget per trade.

### 3.2. Why 100+ Historical Trades Cannot Be Generated Currently
1. An intraday 15m ORB strategy is selective: across a 20-symbol universe, typical breakout frequencies under strict VWAP and RVOL $\ge 1.25$ filters yield between 0.5 and 2 valid setups per calendar day across the entire market.
2. Generating 100+ statistically valid Out-of-Sample trades requires:
   $$\text{Required Trading Days} = \frac{100 \text{ Trades}}{1.5 \text{ Trades/Day}} \times \frac{1}{0.30 \text{ OOS Ratio}} \approx 220 \text{ Trading Days}$$
   This equates to approximately **one full calendar year (250 trading days)** of 1-minute historical candle data across 20 symbols (~90,000 bars per symbol, ~1.8 million bars total).
3. The current database contains 9,500 bars over 2 trading days. It is mathematically impossible to produce 100 historical trades from 2 trading sessions without fabricating data.

## 4. Methodological Guidelines for Future Milestone Research

To maintain strict scientific integrity:
1. **Never Cite Synthetic Results in Performance Marketing:** Any metric derived from `generate_multi_day_dataset()` or `generate_realistic_session_bars()` must be clearly labeled `[SYNTHETIC_STRESS_TEST]`.
2. **Independent Walk-Forward Execution:** Future walk-forward backtests must run exclusively via `scripts/run_live_backtest.py` against `market_candles`.
3. **Formal Statistical Threshold:** AlphaTrade AI v2.0 will automatically classify any backtest with $N < 100$ trades as `INCONCLUSIVE_SAMPLE_SIZE_BELOW_100`.
